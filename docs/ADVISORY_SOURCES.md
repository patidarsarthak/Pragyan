## 1. Primary Agronomic Package of Practices (PoP)

1. **Jawaharlal Nehru Krishi Vishwa Vidyalaya (JNKVV), Jabalpur (MP)**
   - *Document:* "Package of Practices for Rabi Crops (Wheat, Chickpea, Mustard)"
   - *URL:* http://www.jnkvv.org/PDF/PoP_Rabi.pdf
   - *Parameters Derived:* Sowing windows for Malwa/Vindhyan zones, GDD base temperatures ($4.5^\circ\text{C}$ wheat, $8.0^\circ\text{C}$ chickpea), critical irrigation timings (CRI at 21 DAS).
   - *Date Accessed:* 2026-10-04

2. **Rajmata Vijayaraje Scindia Krishi Vishwa Vidyalaya (RVSKVV), Gwalior (MP)**
   - *Document:* "Package of Practices for Kharif Crops of Western Madhya Pradesh (Soybean, Maize, Cotton)"
   - *URL:* http://www.rvskvv.net/extension/crop-production-technology/
   - *Parameters Derived:* Soybean flowering & pod-fill phenology, Nimar cotton thermal thresholds, vertisol drainage bunding guidelines.
   - *Date Accessed:* 2026-10-04

3. **ICAR - Indian Institute of Soybean Research (IISR), Indore (MP)**
   - *Document:* Technical Bulletin No. 42: "Soybean Production Technology for Central India"
   - *URL:* https://iisrindore.icar.gov.in/pdfdoc/TechnicalBulletin42.pdf
   - *Parameters Derived:* Sowing window (June 20 - July 10), flower drop heat sensitivity ($>34^\circ\text{C}$), soil moisture depletion limits.
   - *Date Accessed:* 2026-10-04

4. **ICAR - Indian Institute of Pulses Research (IIPR), Kanpur**
   - *Document:* "Agro-Techniques for Enhancing Chickpea Productivity in Central Zone"
   - *URL:* https://iipr.icar.gov.in/publications/chickpea_bulletin.pdf
   - *Parameters Derived:* Chickpea base temperature ($8.0^\circ\text{C}$), collar rot sensitivity to water stagnation during flowering.
   - *Date Accessed:* 2026-10-04

5. **ICAR - Directorate of Rapeseed-Mustard Research (DRMR), Bharatpur**
   - *Document:* "Package of Practices for Indian Mustard (*Brassica juncea*)"
   - *URL:* https://drmr.res.in/pop_mustard.pdf
   - *Parameters Derived:* Mustard base temperature ($5.0^\circ\text{C}$), aphid infestation weather pre-conditions ($T \le 26^\circ\text{C}$, $\text{RH} \ge 75\%$).
   - *Date Accessed:* 2026-10-04

## 2. Soil & Hydrology Reference Standards

6. **FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper No. 56**
   - *Document:* "Crop Evapotranspiration: Guidelines for computing crop water requirements"
   - *Authors:* Allen, R.G., Pereira, L.S., Raes, D., Smith, M. (1998, FAO Rome)
   - *Parameters Derived:* Penman-Monteith ET0 derivation equations, crop coefficients ($K_c$) by stage (Table 12), Management Allowed Depletion fractions (MAD).
   - *Date Accessed:* 2026-10-04

7. **ICAR - National Bureau of Soil Survey and Land Use Planning (NBSS&LUP), Nagpur**
   - *Document:* "Soils of Madhya Pradesh for Land-Use Planning" (NBSS Publ. 59)
   - *Parameters Derived:* District-level soil orders (Vertisols in Malwa, Inceptisols in Vindhyan, Alfisols in Rewa), field capacities ($150\text{--}185\text{ mm}$ in 60cm root zone), wilting points ($60\text{--}85\text{ mm}$).
   - *Date Accessed:* 2026-10-04

## 3. Operational Agrometeorological Warning Standards

8. **India Meteorological Department (IMD) / ICAR Agromet Advisory Services (AAS)**
   - *Document:* "Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for District and Block Agromet Advisory Bulletins"
   - *URL:* https://mausam.imd.gov.in/imd_latest/contents/agromet_bulletin.php
   - *Parameters Derived:* Rainfall hazard categories (Heavy $\ge 64.5\text{ mm}$, Very Heavy $\ge 115.6\text{ mm}$), heatwave and coldwave definitions.
   - *Date Accessed:* 2026-10-04

9. **Central Insecticides Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) / ICAR-NCIPM**
   - *Document:* "Standard Operational Guidelines for Safe Foliar Application"
   - *Parameters Derived:* Chemical drift threshold (withhold spraying when wind $\ge 15\text{ km/h}$ / $4.17\text{ m/s}$ or active rain $\ge 2.5\text{ mm}$); link to non-chemical IPM cultural practices.
   - *Date Accessed:* 2026-10-04
